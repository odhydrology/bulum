get_wy(dates: Index | list[str | datetime64], wy_month=7, using_end_year=False) → list[int]

Returns water years for a given array of dates. Use this to add water year info into a pandas DataFrame.

Parameters:

  • wy_month (int, default 7)

  • using_end_year (bool, default False) –

    • False aligns water years with the primary water allocation at the start of the water year.

    • True follows the convention used for fiscal years whereby water years are labelled based on their end dates. Using the fiscal convention, the 2022 water year is from 2021-07-01 to 2022-06-30 inclusive.

Returns:

The water years corresponding to the given dates.

Return type:

list of int

Examples

Modified excerpt from bulum.stats.aggregate_stats

>>> df.groupby(get_wy(df.index, wy_month)).sum().median()
